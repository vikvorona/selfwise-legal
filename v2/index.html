What a look check tells you

A short written take on today's photo, in plain words. The scores describe today's look, meaning your choices and styling, and never you as a person.

  • Hair 88
  • Polish 82
  • Skin 79
  • Style 84

Example scores

An overall look score and four scores

Hair, Polish, Skin and Style, each with a sentence explaining it. Skin is about how it looks on camera, nothing more.

It notices what changed

The assistant compares today's photo with your previous one, so it picks up the trim, the new glasses or the different parting.

One to three style tips

Small, specific things to try, based on what is in the photo.

A "looks like" age

A playful guess from the photo. Take it as a bit of fun.

One look, put together for you

Every look check ends with a Look of the Day: a single coordinated look made of two to four changes that go together, described in words and chosen for the person in the photo.

It can draw on hair, facial hair, glasses, accessories and makeup, and it only suggests what applies to you.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone

Selfwise has no account and never asks for your name or email. The app is available in more than 30 languages, and the assistant writes in the one you choose.

Read the privacy policy
  • Stored on your deviceSelfies, try-on previews and look check results are kept in the app on your phone.
