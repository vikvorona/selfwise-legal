Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Your photos, try-on previews and look check results are stored on your device. Photos are sent to our servers and our AI providers only to create your look check or preview, and our servers do not keep them.

Introduction

Selfwise ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Selfwise is a style app. It gives AI-written feedback on how your look comes across in a photo and lets you preview style changes on that photo. Selfwise is for entertainment and personal style inspiration and does not give health or medical advice.

Information We Collect

Photos

  • Selfies: You take a daily selfie using the app's camera feature
  • Storage: Your photos are stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage
  • Processing: When you take a photo or ask for a preview, the photo is sent to our servers, which pass it to our AI providers (see Third-Party Services) to check that the photo is usable, write your look check, or create a try-on preview. Our servers discard the photo once the request is finished
  • We do not store your photos on our servers or in any cloud storage of ours

AI-Generated Previews

  • What they are: Try-on previews are images created by AI from a photo you took in the app and a description of the change (a ready-made style, an idea from your look check, or text you type)
  • Storage: Generated images are returned to the app and stored locally on your device. We do not keep a copy

Style Profile and Look Check Results

  • Style profile: Optional preferences you enter (style goals, age range, gender, preferred styles, areas you want help with, and the tone and detail of feedback you prefer) are stored on your device. They are sent along with a look check so the feedback can be tailored to you
  • Results: The commentary, scores, "looks like" age, tips and suggested looks from each look check are stored on your device

Usage Data

  • App user ID: A random identifier created by the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and the app has no account or sign-in. The same identifier is used for your subscription (see RevenueCat). It is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device, so it can remain after the app is deleted and be reused if you reinstall
  • Usage records: For each request, our servers record against that identifier: which feature was used, the time, how long the request took, whether it succeeded, and whether you were on the free or Premium tier. For a look check they also record the scores and the "looks like" age that were returned. For a try-on preview they also record the category of the change (for example "haircut")
  • Preview counts: Our servers keep a count of the try-on previews made with your identifier per day and per month, to apply the Premium preview limits
  • Server logs: Our servers write technical logs that contain the app user ID, a request ID and processing statistics
  • Error and crash reports: The app sends error and crash reports and a sample of performance measurements to Sentry (see Third-Party Services)
  • Not included: Usage records, preview counts and server logs do not contain your photos, generated images, style profile or the text of your look checks

We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

How We Use Your Information

  • Photo check: Each new selfie is sent to our AI provider to confirm it is clear enough to use.
  • Daily look check: Your selfie, your most recent earlier selfie (so changes can be noticed), a short summary of up to five recent look checks (dates, scores, tips and suggested looks), your style profile and your chosen language are sent to our AI provider to write the feedback. Images are processed in real time and are not stored by us.
  • Try-on previews: The photo you choose and a description of the change are sent to our AI provider to generate a preview image.
  • Subscriptions and limits: Our servers check with RevenueCat whether your identifier has an active Premium subscription, and use the preview counts to apply the daily and monthly limits.
  • App improvement: Usage records and error reports help us fix bugs, monitor cost and reliability, and improve the service.

Data Storage and Security

Local-First Architecture

  • Your photos, generated previews, look check results and preferences are stored on your device
  • We use the local storage provided by iOS
  • Our app does not upload your personal data to a cloud backup of ours. If you use your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup), it may include the app's data, under Apple's terms

Data Transmission

  • Photos are transmitted securely via HTTPS for AI processing
  • Photos are processed in memory and never written to disk on our servers
  • Requests carry the random app user ID and do not include your name, email or other contact details. The app user ID is not passed on to the AI providers
  • As with any internet service, our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services) processes your IP address in order to deliver requests. We do not add it to your usage records

Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude)

  • Processes your selfies, the summary of recent look checks and your style profile to check the photo and write your daily look check
  • Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy and its commercial terms, under which content sent through its API is not used to train its models

Google Gemini AI

  • Processes the photo you choose and the description of the change to generate try-on preview images
  • Subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • We use Google Gemini's paid API tier, under which user content is not used to improve Google's products or train its AI models
  • Versions 1.x of the app also use Google Gemini, instead of Anthropic, to analyse the selfie

Amazon Web Services

  • Hosts our servers and the database that holds usage records and preview counts
  • Subject to the AWS Privacy Notice

RevenueCat

  • Manages subscription purchases and tells the app and our servers whether a subscription is active
  • Receives the app user ID and your purchase information from Apple. Does not receive any photo data
  • Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy

Sentry

  • Receives error and crash reports and sampled performance data, including device model, iOS version and app version
  • We do not send photo files to Sentry. An error report can include a screenshot of the app screen at the moment of the error, which may show a photo that was on screen
  • Subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy

Apple

  • Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details

Your Rights and Choices

Data Control

  • Delete previews: You can delete any generated preview from within the app
  • Replace today's photo: Taking a new photo replaces the current day's photo and results
  • Reset your profile: You can reset your style profile and preferences on the profile screen
  • Disable notifications: Daily reminders are scheduled on your device and are optional. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings
  • Delete all local data: Uninstalling the app removes the locally stored photos, previews and results
  • Usage records: Contact us with your app user ID (shown in Settings) to request access to, or deletion of, the usage records and preview counts linked to it

Opt-Out Options

  • The style profile is optional
  • Camera access is requested only when you take a photo
  • Notifications are optional

Children's Privacy

Selfwise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

Data Retention

  • Photos and generated previews: Stored on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • Preferences and results: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app
  • Usage records: Deleted automatically from our servers one year after they are created
  • Preview counts and server logs: Kept on our servers for as long as they are needed to apply your limits and operate the service, or until you ask us to delete them
  • Crash reports: Retained by Sentry for 90 days

International Users

Our servers are located in the European Union (Frankfurt). By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your photos to our servers for AI processing. Our AI providers and other service providers listed above may process data in other countries, including the United States.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
